# Image-generation prompt for cut-out characters (ChatGPT)

Attach `docs/style-reference.png` along with the prompt. Paste the whole block. To make another character, change only
the CHARACTER paragraph.

---

I'm attaching a style reference: frames from my animated horror shorts. Match THAT style exactly. It's a flat, simple
2D cartoon made of basic vector shapes, like cut-paper puppets. It is NOT anime, NOT semi-realistic digital painting,
NOT concept art and NOT a fashion illustration.

STYLE RULES (follow all of them, in every image):
- Build everything from a few big, simple, flat shapes with smooth curved edges, like vector shapes in Illustrator.
  You should be able to count the shapes on the face.
- Solid flat colour fills. At most two tones per area: one base colour plus one hard-edged darker shadow shape. No
  soft airbrush shading, no gradients on skin, no blending, no painterly texture, no brush strokes, no grain.
- No outlines, or at most a very thin dark outline. No sketch lines, no hatching.
- Face: very simplified. Skin is one flat shape, an oval face. The eyes are two dark almond shapes with a tiny
  highlight dot: no iris detail, no eyelashes, no realistic eyelids. No nose, or a tiny single line. The lips are one
  small flat red shape. No cheekbones, no contouring, no blush, no pores, no realistic eyebrows (two simple dark strokes
  at most). Ears hidden by the hair. NOT pointed ears.
- Hair: one or two big solid black shapes with a single lighter rim-light edge. No individual strands.
- Hands: simple mitten-like cartoon hands with four simple fingers. Nails are small flat red shapes. No knuckles, veins
  or wrinkles.
- Clothes: big flat black shapes. Show lace ONLY as a simple scalloped edge (a row of half-circles) at the cuffs, collar
  and hem. No lace pattern, no floral detail, no embroidery, no fabric folds beyond one or two simple shadow shapes.
- Proportions: slightly stylised and simple, a slightly larger head, a simple straight-sided body, like a paper puppet.
  Not a realistic or fashion-model figure.
- Lighting: one cool bluish rim light on the left edges only, drawn as a thin flat lighter band. No glow, no halo, no
  bloom, no light spilling around the body, no vignette.
- Palette: pale bluish-white skin (#e4e6ee), near-black clothes and hair (#0a0a0e), dark red lips and nails
  (#9a0c1c), a cool rim light (#9fb4cf). Nothing else.
- Background: completely transparent (PNG), or flat pure white if transparency is impossible. No dark backdrop, no
  shadow on the ground, no glow.
- Keep it charming and readable at phone size. If in doubt, SIMPLIFY.

CHARACTER: a young pale vampire woman. Long straight black hair. Very pale bluish skin. Dark almond eyes. Small dark red
lips. A black long-sleeved knee-length dress with scalloped lace edges at the cuffs, collar and hem. Simple black boots
to below the knee with flat heels. Calm, unreadable expression.

VIEW: three-quarter view facing left, the same in every image.

Generate SEPARATE images, one per item, all at the same scale and in the same style:
1. Full body, standing straight, arms hanging slightly away from the body, mouth closed. This is the master; every
   other image must match it.
2. Head with hair and neck, with a BLANK face (skin only: no eyes, brows or mouth).
3. Torso with the dress, no head, arms or legs. Rounded extra shapes at the neck and shoulders for overlap.
4. Left upper arm with sleeve, rounded at both ends.
5. Left forearm with sleeve and lace cuff, rounded at both ends.
6. Left hand, open.
7. Left hand, closed and gripping.
8. Right upper arm with sleeve, rounded at both ends.
9. Right forearm with sleeve and lace cuff, rounded at both ends.
10. Right hand, open.
11. Right hand, pointing.
12. Left leg with boot, rounded top.
13. Right leg with boot, rounded top.
14. Eyes open (pair, sized for the blank face in image 2).
15. Eyes half closed.
16. Eyes closed (two simple curved lines).
17. Eyes wide, with big black pupils.
18. Eyes glowing red (flat red almond shapes).
19. Eyebrows neutral (two simple strokes).
20. Eyebrows raised.
21. Eyebrows angry.
22. Mouth closed.
23. Mouth with a slight smile.
24. Mouth open small ("ah").
25. Mouth open round ("oh").
26. Mouth open showing two small fangs.
27. Mouth sad.

Every part must use exactly the same flat colours, the same rim light on the left, and the same simple shapes, so the
pieces fit together like a paper puppet.
