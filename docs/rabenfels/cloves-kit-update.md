# Cloves kit additions

ClovesCharacters.tsx adds original Safwan and unnamed witness profile/three-quarter art with fixed-length two-bone limbs, authored foot targets, head/eye/mouth acting and a separate open-claw hand. ClovesProps.tsx adds cloves, a candle watch lantern, torn wool coat and wash basin. TavernLanding.tsx adds a Broken Bell public stair angle and uses the same staircase component for its real and mirrored view. Reflected humans are supplied explicitly; vampire layers are excluded.

These extend the existing kit rather than restyling its shared characters/fonts/lighting. New panels appear in the regenerated kit catalog; prior episodes remain registered. New episode-local cobbles, shrouded body and locked-shop detail remain in Investigation.tsx. The public generated town plate is the final approved weathered Part I asset; no new image generation was required.

After user review, Safwan's far eye is narrowed and his side ear moves with the head turn and renders before the eyes. scripts/revise_cloves_voice.py supplies clear regular stranger dialogue, and sound_lib.short_room adds restrained early reflections. assemble_cloves.py now remeasures current takes including the spoken decision opening.
