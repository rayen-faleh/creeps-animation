# Rabenfels (imported project)

The owner's Rabenfels series (YOU ARE THE VAMPIRE · Rabenfels, 1732), built in a separate workspace and imported here
as-is from `rabenfels-part-two-project-and-kit.zip`. It is a self-contained Remotion project with its own `package.json`,
`src/kit`, `scripts/` and `public/`. It does not share this repo's root `src/kit`.

- Start with `RABENFELS-SOURCE-ONLY.md`: the story map, plus how to regenerate the audio (no audio or video was in the zip).
- Canon: `story/rabenfels-series-bible-and-pilot.md` (master) and `src/episodes/rabenfels-02/series-ledger.json` (latest
  world state).
- Episodes: `src/episodes/rabenfels-01` (Night One) and `src/episodes/rabenfels-02` (Part II: Cloves).
- Town plates: `public/episodes/rabenfels-01/art/town-night.png` and `town-dawn.png`.
- The owner's final renders of both parts are kept locally in `out/rabenfels/` (git-ignored).
- Part II ends before the stranger touches Safwan. Part III starts from the next winning comment:
  "You decided to <winning action>."
