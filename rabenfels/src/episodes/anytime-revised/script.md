# Anytime — revised narration

Your friend stopped knocking.

That's your fault.

Remember their first visit?

They waited at your open door.

You said, come in. Anytime.

Now they do.

Tonight, you woke with a hand around your wrist.

Your friend's voice, inches from your ear.

You said anytime.

You switched on the lamp.

In the mirror, you could see your wrist.

But not the hand around it.

Then it pulled.
