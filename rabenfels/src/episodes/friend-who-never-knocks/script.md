# The Friend Who Never Knocks

If your friend never knocks, check the mirror.

Remember their first visit?

They waited outside, even though the door was open.

You laughed.

Just come in. You don't have to ask.

They never asked again.

Last night, you woke with their hand around your wrist.

You said I could come in.

You reached for the lamp.

They held on.

In the mirror, your arm was hanging in mid-air.

Alone.

Then their fingers tightened.
