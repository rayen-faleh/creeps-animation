## Why this ending

The earlier ending established that the witness expected Elias. This revision reveals another vampire and places a named human in immediate danger. The antagonist also knows Elias has something to hide. The next comment can propose a concrete plan, with both a person to protect and a secret at risk.

The question invites solutions rather than prescribing two outcomes. A viewer might try a distraction, bargain, summon help, exploit the room, expose the stranger or sacrifice Elias's cover. These are possibilities, not a preselected vote or guaranteed successful actions.

## Draft continuity changes

These are proposed story additions; no new episode has been published and no following winner has been selected.

- The corpse's collar smells of cloves. Otto uses cloves to mask the smell of the animal blood he sells Elias. This is an original fictional detail, not a historical or medical claim.
- Elias can recognise a strong familiar scent at close range. The scent alone neither identifies a murderer nor enables an infallible supernatural trail.
- Safwan finds black wool beneath the victim's fingernails. Marta says the black coat she is washing belongs to the witness.
- Otto's shop is locked. His location and involvement are unknown.
- The witness is visibly without a reflection and therefore another vampire under the established series rules. His identity, involvement in the death, relationship with Otto and reason for targeting Safwan are unresolved.
- The stranger's demand establishes that he knows Elias has a secret. Do not invent an earlier meeting, ancient feud, new power or guaranteed promise-keeping to explain it prematurely.
- Safwan hears the threat and asks what secret the stranger means. He has not seen Elias's teeth or knowingly identified Elias as a vampire.
- Marta's knowledge remains unresolved. Her glance identifies the guest; it does not establish that she knew his nature or deliberately endangered Safwan.
- The encounter remains in the tavern's public room and stair landing. Elias has not entered the guest's private room; no new invitation is assumed.
- Matthias is still preparing hunters' blades. Grete expects an answer before dawn. Elias's animal-blood diet leaves him weaker than human feeding; no exact strength comparison with this stranger has been established.
- No grab, injury, death, feeding, rescue or public exposure occurs before the cut. The next selected action must be performed or meaningfully attempted, with human characters retaining their own motives and agency.
- Safwan persists as a resident. His peril is not a prewritten death or an automatic penalty for being the winning commenter.
