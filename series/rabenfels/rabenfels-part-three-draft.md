## What the action costs

The winning action works: Safwan sees the stairs empty in the mirror and understands what the stranger is. But a mirror
is also the one thing that can expose Elias. Every beat after the reveal tightens on that. The cliffhanger is the
mirror turning on Elias. It's mute-readable: in the glass, Safwan's hand grips nothing where he is holding Elias's arm.

### For first-time viewers

- L00 plus the mirror image explains the premise instantly: someone isn't in the mirror.
- L08 and L09 say outright what both men are, without the narrator having to.
- The ending is a concrete problem anyone can answer: lie, confess, smash the mirror, distract him, send him after the
  stranger…

### Rules respected

- **Reflections:** Elias is never reflected. His step "out of the glass" matters because only being in the mirror's
  view can prove absence. When Safwan pulls him back into view, the glass shows the empty grip.
- **Invitation:** the stranger escapes into his private guest room. Elias has no invitation and can't follow.
- **Agency:** Safwan acts on his own (whistle, grabbing Elias, his question). The stranger chooses retreat over a fight
  because the watch is coming.
- **No cheat:** nothing erases memory; the next winner must deal with what Safwan saw.

## Ledger changes (proposed)

- Safwan has seen that the stranger has no reflection, and knows the stranger is a vampire.
- He suspects Elias knew in advance. He has not yet noticed Elias's own missing reflection (the grip is in the glass;
  he's looking at Elias).
- Safwan blew the watch whistle. The watch (Captain Grete) is on the way.
- The stranger is in his rented room above the Broken Bell. It is a private room; Elias has no invitation.
- The stranger has told Elias, in front of Safwan, that Safwan will work out what Elias is.
- Still unresolved: Otto's whereabouts, the killer, what Marta knows.

## Round 2: more tension, more events, more curiosity (owner's notes)

Changes from round 1:
- **Curiosity hook after the credit line:** "It worked. That was the problem." First-time viewers hear the outcome is
  bad before they know why.
- **An Otto clue mid-episode:** as the stranger flees, Otto's shop key (on a cleaver-shaped tag) drops from his coat.
  Why does he have it? This connects Part II's chained shop and raises a new question without answering it.
- **An eventful, escalating ending:** after the empty grip in the glass, the watch bursts in: Captain Grete, three
  lanterns. She asks Safwan, "Where is he?" Safwan opens his mouth, and looks at the mirror. Who will he name, the
  stranger upstairs or you? Three people, one mirror, and lantern light about to hit it.
