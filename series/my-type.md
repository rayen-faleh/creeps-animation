# MY TYPE: a vampire horror-comedy with a council vote (brainstorm, round 1)

Owner's idea: Hollywood made vampires attractive, which shaped the narrator's taste in women. He spends years at goth
nights, finally meets a real vampire, gets bitten by accident and turned, and now a council that runs the city decides
whether he lives.

## Angle

- **Hook:** a flash-forward. Open in the council room mid-crisis ("seven vampires are voting on whether I live"), then
  jump back to the comedy of how he got there. The in-progress crisis hook from the playbook.
- **Tone:** like *Dating a Vampire*. Funny about his type and his blindness to the signs, then cold once the envelope
  arrives.
- **Folklore and lore viewers can argue about:**
  - She has to be invited in.
  - She doesn't drink at the bar.
  - She doesn't show up in pictures.
  - The ruling council is modern lore (Rice's covens, *True Blood*'s Authority, *Underworld*), kept generic here: "the
    council".
- **Twist:** the crime on trial isn't the bite. **He posted proof.** The selfie story from that night shows two glasses
  and nobody next to him, and three hundred people watched it.
- **Clues, planted before the turn:**
  - Visual: we see him take the selfie at the wine beat, and the screen shows only him.
  - Spoken: "she was not drinking" and "asked me to invite her in".
- **Ending:** the vote is three each, and the last vote is hers. She hasn't looked at him once. The whisper is
  "Sorry.", echoing "She said sorry. She said it a lot."
- **Send-worthy:** "Hates mornings" as a type; the empty selfie; "I thought that was flirting."

## Ending options

1. **Poll (recommended, as in *Graveyard Shift*):** an end card, "You're her. SPARE or STAKE?". Part 2 plays the winning branch.
2. **One-off:** cut on the hovering hand, with no follow-up.

## Where Parts 2-3 could go (if poll)

- **SPARE:** he lives, on one condition. Everyone who watched the story has to be dealt with. "Three hundred and
  twelve. Thirty of them were at that goth night." He has to choose between his old friends and the council.
- **STAKE:** she votes stake, then the twist: the council's stake is a sentence, not a weapon, and it's served by the
  one who made him. She gets him out of the city before dawn, and now both of them are hunted.
- **Thread for later:** "She said sorry. She said it a lot." Was it really an accident? How many "accidents" has she had?
