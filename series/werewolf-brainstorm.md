# Werewolf horror series: brainstorm

Format (fixed by gothic-pov-style): 9:16 reels, 60–65 s, first-person POV with only the narrator's hands visible, moonlit gothic house, `bm_lewis` narrator, serif captions with one red word per reveal, hard cut to black and a blackletter title card.

Every episode uses a real, nameable piece of werewolf folklore. Viewers should be able to look it up and argue about it in the comments.

---

## Concept A (recommended): **SAME WOUND**

**Folklore:** wound a werewolf and the person wakes up with the same wound. This is the repercussion legend. The best-known version is the 16th-century Auvergne tale: a hunter cuts the paw off a wolf, the paw becomes a woman's hand wearing a ring, and the lord's wife turns up missing that hand.

**Why it's relatable:** a grandparent who moves in with you, house rules from childhood you never questioned, the neighbour's dog going missing, a ring that's always on someone's finger.

**Series spine:** Gran's rules kept him safe as a child. Each episode he breaks one, and each reveal turns one of her protections into something else. The final turn of the series is that her rules were never protecting *him* from wolves. They were protecting everyone else from him.

The twist reads with the sound off: a ring on the sill, a bandaged hand with no ring.

### Episode arc

1. **The Latch.** Gran is the wolf. (Rule: never hurt a wolf at your window.)
2. **Silver.** He sews silver into Gran's pillow to keep her down. The next morning the burn is on *his* palm. (Rule: silver burns the wolf.) It plants the idea that the wound goes to someone else.
3. **The Clothes.** In Marie de France's *Bisclavret* (12th century), a werewolf who loses his hidden clothes can't change back. He finds where Gran hides a bundle every month: a man's clothes in his size, folded the night before *his* birthday each year.
4. **The Dogs.** Footage from the neighbour's doorbell camera, shown only as phone glow on his hands. The dogs weren't taken. They were following someone home, and it was him.
5. **Same Wound (finale).** The latch wound from episode 1 was never only Gran's. He looks down at his own knuckles: an old scar in the exact shape of the casement latch, there since he was eight. She took the blow for him that first night, and every night after.

The episode 5 reveal recasts episode 1. Gran's promise ("never hurt a wolf at your window") was there so he would never find out which side of the window he was on.

## Concept B: **THREE TIMES**

**Folklore:** call a werewolf by its baptismal name three times and it turns back into a person (European, including French loup-garou traditions).

**Hook:** "Every full moon, Mum said my name through my bedroom door. Three times."

As a child he thought she was checking he was asleep. Now he lives alone and nobody says his name. Ep1 reveal: he wakes with the phone glowing in his hand, a voicemail from Mum timed 00:03 that just says his name three times, and his hand holding the phone isn't his (creature-hand curl). Series hook: who keeps calling, and what happens the night nobody does?

**Relatable:** parents' bedtime rituals, a voicemail from Mum, living alone for the first time.

## Concept C: **THE DOG KNOWS**

**Folklore:** dogs sense werewolves and refuse to sleep near them.

**Hook:** "Biscuit has slept on my bed for nine years. Tonight he won't come in."

He assumes something is in the room. Torch search, scratching under the bed. Ep1 reveal: the dog isn't scared of the room. When the torch comes back to the door, the dog is lying across the threshold, facing *him*, guarding the hallway where his little sister sleeps. The proof image is his own hand gripping the bedframe, with nails sunk into the wood.

**Relatable:** a pet that suddenly acts strange, the most shareable kind of horror.

## Why A fits the kit best

The gothic-pov kit already has an `elder` hand style, `ElderProfile`, `Duvet`, `WindowWall` with a sill and a casement that swings, `Torch`, `Eyelids` and the creature hand. Episode 1 needs three new pieces: a **wolf paw** gripping the sill (the kit's `creature` hand is a grey vampire hand, so a furred paw style is new art), a ring on the sill, and a bandage overlay on the elder hand. `GranSilhouette` and the elder-hand style already cover Gran.
