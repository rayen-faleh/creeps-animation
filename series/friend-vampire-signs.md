# "You could be friends with a vampire without knowing it" — signs reel (brainstorm)

A list-of-signs reel in our gothic style. Each sign gets one shot and one line; they start so ordinary they could be
anyone you know, and get harder to explain away. Second person, deadpan `bm_lewis`, a hint of comedy, a creepy turn
at the end, and a send-worthy button.

## Method (from our research)
- **Escalation as the loop ladder**: subtle (1–3) → odd (4–6) → hard to explain (7–9) → the turn (10). A big sign
  number on screen ("SIGN 4") carries it on mute and promises more.
- **Benign violation**: early signs are relatable habits (safe), each with a folklore root fans will spot; the last
  one removes the safety.
- **Specific and deadpan beats generic**: "the long way round the river" not "avoids water"; "every time" not "often".
- **Send-worthy by design**: viewers will tag the friend who fits. The ending turns that into the twist.
- **Lore roots** (fans comment on these): invitation; garlic; counting compulsion; running water (in Stoker, the Count
  can cross only at slack or flood tide); cold skin; no reflection / not in pictures; agelessness; sleeping by day.

## Draft script (~140 words, ~60 s + end card)

| # | Line | Folklore root | Picture |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L01 | You could be friends with a vampire and never notice. Start with the small things. | | Mute-proof hook: a group selfie on a phone: four friends, and a hand holding the phone that belongs to no one in the picture. |
| S1 | Sign one. They always offer to take the group photo. | not in pictures | The friend's pale hand holding the phone up. "SIGN 1". |
| S2 | Sign two. "Not a morning person." They've said it every morning since you met. | sleeping by day | Text thread: "can't do brunch x" stacked, dated over years. |
| S3 | Sign three. At your door, they wait until you say come in. Every single time. | invitation | Your doorstep at night; they stand back from the threshold, smiling. |
| S4 | Sign four. The garlic bread arrives. They suddenly need the loo. | garlic | A basket of garlic bread; their chair, empty, still turning. |
| S5 | Sign five. They tidy the sugar sachets. Then they count them. Twice. | counting | Pale fingers lining up sachets in rows of ten. |
| S6 | Sign six. They'll walk twenty minutes round the river rather than cross the bridge. | running water | A footbridge; their footprints veer away along the bank. |
| S7 | Sign seven. Cold hands. "Bad circulation." For eleven years. | cold skin | Your hand in theirs; frost creeps from their fingertips. |
| S8 | Sign eight. They talk about the eighties like they were there. You're both twenty-six. | agelessness | A record sleeve; their finger tapping a date: 1983. |
| S9 | Sign nine. You've never been to their place. Not once. | (invitation, reversed) | Their front door at the end of a dark street, curtains shut. |
| S10 | And sign ten. They're the one who sent you this video. | the turn | Your phone: a message from them with this video's link, "thought of you x". Then three dots: typing. |
| — | Whisper: *"Can I come in?"* | | Hard cut. |
| — | End card: **TAG THE FRIEND WHO TAKES ALL THE PHOTOS** / "How many signs does yours have?" | | |

- Red words: one per beat, e.g. `twice.` (S5), `once.` (S9), `video.` (S10).
- Comedy beats: S2, S4, S8. Creep beats: S3, S7, S9, S10.

## Alternatives to discuss
- **Number of signs**: 10 (above), or 7 for a tighter ~45 s (drop S2, S6, S8).
- **Ending**: the meta turn (they sent you this video), or a reversal ("…and sign ten: you've started taking all the
  photos.": *you're* the one turning), or the sleepover ("Tonight, for the first time, they've invited you round.").
- **Voice**: deadpan `bm_lewis` talking to you, or `bf_emma` as a friend telling you about *her* friend.
- **Format**: a one-off, or a recurring "signs" series (your neighbour is a werewolf; your house has a ghost; your
  flatmate is a witch) reusing a SIGN counter and the tag-a-friend end card.
