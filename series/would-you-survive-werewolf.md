# "Would you survive?" — Werewolf (30 s short, brainstorm)

Second episode of the format (`series/would-you-survive-format.md`). Same beats as the vampire:
hook → Day one (small inconvenience) → Day two (deadly rule, solved too easily: "Easy.") → Day three (the rule
nobody thinks about bites) → one-line modern punchline with a red word → phone sting → whisper → end card.

The vampire's engine was **an old rule meeting an ordinary modern system** (invitations meet renting). The werewolf
needs its own: here the system is **surveillance**: doorbell cameras and street group chats.

## Three concepts

### A. "Except your doorbell." (recommended)
Full moon on Day two: you lock yourself in and throw the key out of the window. Easy. Day three you wake on your own
doorstep, door hanging open, mud everywhere. Nobody saw a thing. **Except your doorbell.** And it has already
shared the clip with the street's group chat.
- Send-worthy: everyone has a neighbour who posts doorbell clips; "the werewolf's real enemy is the street WhatsApp".
- Mute proof: a phone notification "Front Door · Motion 02:14" with a thumbnail of a wolf on *your* step; the chat.
- Mirrors the vampire: the same doorstep set, the same phone, a new monster. It feels like a series.

### B. "Every filling in your mouth."
Silver burns werewolves. Day one, your ring blisters your finger. Day two, full moon, locked in, easy. Day three, the
toothache. You open wide in the mirror: **amalgam fillings, part silver.** Sting: a text, "Dentist tomorrow: two more fillings."
- True and surprising (amalgam fillings do contain silver), big comment bait. Weaker on mute (a mouth close-up is hard to read
  at phone size and edges towards body horror).

### C. "Your flatmate has the app."
Day two you lock yourself in your room with a new smart lock. Easy. Day three: the lock has an app, and your flatmate
has it too. Their text: "Locked out lol, letting myself in x". Cliffhanger at the door at moonrise.
- Relatable, but leans on the viewer knowing smart locks, and the twist is mostly text on a screen.

**Recommendation: A.**

## Production notes

- Reuse: Hand `wolf` style (fur, mud) blending from `adult`, WindowWall (night, open casement), Doorstep + WarmHall,
  MoorStreet, Phone, KitchenTable, DayTag, SurviveCard, MudMarks/claw scratches from Charms, Eyelids for the wake-up.
- New art: **a dog** (side profile only, hackles up: profiles read best at phone size), a **bandage** on the hand,
  a **doorbell camera** with a blinking ring, a **video thumbnail** frame for the phone (a wolf silhouette on the step).
- Grade: cold; Day three opens at grey dawn (the only light change).
- Sound: a dog growl and whimper (synthesised), key turning, key clinking on stone far below, birdsong-free grey morning
  wind, a doorbell chime under the reveal, phone buzzes; your heartbeat this time (you're alive), racing into the knock.
- Whisper options: "Here, boy." (my pick: a neighbour at the door calling the "dog"), "Good boy.", or "Whose dog is this?"
