# THE WITCH BOTTLE — series bible

Pick-up file with every part, script and open decision: `series/the-witch-bottle-parts.md`.

Four ~60 s parts, 9:16. Mardle Fen (fictional), a fen village in East Anglia, winter 1645, at the height of the
English witch panic. Each part is built on one real witch test and stands alone: hook fact → one-line recap over the
bearded jug → the test → a twist inside the test → cliffhanger. Plan: `series/witch-brainstorm.md` (round 2).

## People (all fictional)
- **I** (the narrator): nine that winter; remembers it as a man (bm_lewis). Small hands, brown wool sleeve.
- **Gran**: keeps the house and the old remedies. Profile silhouette with a bun and spectacles; grey homespun sleeve.
- **My sister**: the baby in the hooded cradle, coughing.
- **Goody Ashe**: the widow two doors down; held me when I was born. Voice bf_emma.
- **The Witch-finder**: a title, never a name. Gloved hand, tall hat in profile, a book, a brass needle.

## Canon so far
- **Part I · The Bottle** (done): three babies dead since harvest; my sister coughs; Gran says it's *someone*; the
  bearded jug filled with bent pins, iron nails, a lock of the baby's hair **and one long grey hair from a comb I'd never
  seen**; set in the embers; Gran's rule (the witch feels every pin and must come to the door). Midnight: the baby stops
  coughing; a knock; Gran says open it; a hand bound in smoking rag; "*It burns.*"

## Still to come
- **II · The Needle**: it was Goody Ashe; the Witch-finder pricks for the devil's mark; the needle's point slides back
  into its handle; he catches me holding it: "*Hold out your hand.*"
- **III · The Swimming**: I'm made to hold the rope; she floats because I want her to live; my palms blister in the
  shape of pins in front of the village; smoke rising from our cottage (Gran heating a second bottle). "*It's the child.*"
- **IV · The Prayer**: the Lord's Prayer test; Gran's scarred palms; "the first bottle, I put her hair in. This one, yours."
  Ending open.
