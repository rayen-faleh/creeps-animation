## Facts to keep consistent
- Both boys turn on the full moon. In 2006 Danny attacked Mick; S. was "on the moor till dawn" and remembers nothing.
- Mick wrote one-handed (left) from August 2006; the first entries are shaky.
- Someone left the torch and the key after Mick died: Danny's mum, following the last page (pay off in IV).
- Danny was awake at 03:07 on the full moon in Part I, texting.
- Mick's house is number 11; Danny's is number 6 (red paint), and it HAS silver nails. Mick's sill had eight; Danny pulled them on the night of Part III.
- Danny wants S. to run with him ("Come out."), not to kill him.
- Part IV: Danny's mum (unnamed) keeps the watch at the lamp with Mick's double-barrelled shotgun, silver cartridges. She has watched S. since he was eight. She aims at Danny, not S.: "It runs in our family. Not yours." Whether she fires is unresolved.
