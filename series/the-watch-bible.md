aloud), voiced with Chatterbox from the owner's voice. Mick Ashworth (1949–2026), Danny's one-armed uncle.

## Facts to keep consistent
- Both boys turn on the full moon. In 2006 Danny attacked Mick; S. was "on the moor till dawn" and remembers nothing.
- Mick wrote one-handed (left) from August 2006; the first entries are shaky.
- Someone left the torch and the key after Mick died: Danny's mum, following the last page (pay off in IV).
- Danny was awake at 03:07 on the full moon in Part I, texting.

## Planned
- **III · Silver Nails**: Danny at the door, friendly, explaining; S. checks the street: every house has silver nails except Danny's.
- **IV · The Torch**: Danny's mum has kept the watch since Mick died; she watches Danny's window, armed.
- **V · A Wolf Took It**: the notebooks' oldest page, before 2006: Mick was the first wolf. It runs in Danny's family's blood; S. only has it because Danny bit him the night of the arm. Mick watched S. out of guilt.
