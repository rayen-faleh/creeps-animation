# The Sickle (brief)

- Standalone short, ~56 s, 9:16, built to repeat what made THE COUNT Part I (114k views) work. Analysis and both script
  rounds: `series/count-successor.md`. The owner chose candidate A with THE COUNT's hook shape: "I never believed in
  vampires. But my grandmother did."
- Real folklore: the 17th-century "vampire" burial at Pień, Poland (excavated 2022, face reconstructed 2024). A woman was
  buried with a sickle across her neck and a padlock on her big toe.
- Story: Gran slept with both, "so I stay where I'm put". After she died, the undertaker wouldn't put them in the coffin.
  Then the padlock goes missing from the kitchen drawer, wet bare footprints climb the stairs, and the lock closes on
  your own toe. The twist is that the sickle wasn't keeping anything away from her; it kept her in the ground.
- Copies THE COUNT's craft:

  - a cold present
  - one camera move following the prints from the floor up the stairs to your door
  - the WindowWall bedroom
  - hands that act
  - a mute-readable payoff: the padlock clicking shut on a toe
  - a whispered echo over black (`bf_emma`) before the blackletter title
