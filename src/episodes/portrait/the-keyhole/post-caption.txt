Have you ever woken up and couldn't move? 🗝️

In Scandinavian folklore, that's the mare, the "night-mare". She slips in through keyholes. Plug the hole while she's inside and she can't leave... and in the old legends, she becomes your wife.

Would you pull the wool out? 👇
