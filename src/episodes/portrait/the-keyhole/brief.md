# THE KEYHOLE (brief)

Script and reasoning: `series/the-keyhole.md`. This is a standalone short made after The Shroud Eater stalled at its first test batch.

| Item | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Folklore | The Scandinavian and German **mare** (the "night-mare"): the weight on your chest in sleep paralysis. She enters through keyholes. Plug the hole while she's inside and she's trapped; in the legend she becomes a wife, and leaves when the plug is pulled out. |
| Hook | A brass keyhole plugged with wool that breathes in and out (0–3.7 s). |
| Twist | ~44 s: "I never got rid of the mare. I married her." |
| After the twist | ~7 s: the ring left on the bedside table, her hair falling over the ceiling, then a whisper ("You let me out", `af_nicole`). |
| Safety | No death, bodies, coffins, blood or injury. The mare is never drawn: she is breath, pressure on the duvet, cold light and hair. No faces. |
| Pace | 20 shots in 51 s. The keyhole recurs as the anchor (hook, Gran, me, morning, pull-out, cold open hole). |
| Grades | Candle amber (Gran), warm morning (meeting her), moonlit blue (paralysis), cold keyhole light (the end). |
| Voice | Kokoro `bm_lewis`. The whisper is `af_nicole` at speed 0.9, whisper-processed. |
| Sound | The earshot kit (wool pushes and pulls, bed springs, the draught, the ring, impacts) plus a synthesised breath through the keyhole, Gran's music box (detuned when he meets his wife), ticks and heartbeats. |
