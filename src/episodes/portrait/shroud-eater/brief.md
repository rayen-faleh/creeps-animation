# THE SHROUD EATER (brief)

A standalone short in THE COUNT, Part I's mould. Analysis and script: `series/the-shroud-eater.md`.

| Item | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Hook | "In my family, we bury our dead with a stone in their mouth." Gran's hand lowers a river stone to Grandad's lips over the coffin rim, in candlelight. |
| Folklore | The Nachzehrer, or shroud-eater (German and Kashubian folklore): the dead chew their shroud and the family dies one by one. A stone in the mouth stops it. The 2009 "Vampire of Venice" was found with a brick in her mouth. |
| Misdirect | "Another funeral" is never said to be his. Viewers suspect whoever was buried (the commenters' "I thought Gran would be the vampire"). |
| Twist | He is the one in the coffin, chewing. Mum didn't believe, so there was no stone. The proof works on mute: quilted satin six inches above his face. One line follows: "It was my funeral." |
| Clues | Spoken: "another funeral", "I can't seem to get up", "coming from the grave". Visual: Mum's text "Same as you.", grey hands, the candle row. |
| After the twist | About 7 s, adding only a consequence: Mum's "I'll come and see you tomorrow x", the last candle, and the whisper "Still hungry" (an echo of "the dead wake up hungry"). |
| Pace | 22 shots in 61 s. A new image or action every 2 s or less. |
| Faces | None. Silhouettes, hands, objects, phone screens. |
| Grades | Candle amber (past), rain grey, phone glow, torch white. |
| Voice | Kokoro `bm_lewis`. The whisper is the narrator's own voice, processed. |
| Sound | The code-made earshot kit (`docs/sound-kit.md`): rain, soil on the lid, candle snuffs, phone buzzes, chewing, the torch switch, impacts. Plus the house drone, tension, heartbeat and music box. |
