In my family, we bury our dead with a stone in their mouth. 🪦

The Nachzehrer, or "shroud-eater", is real German folklore: the dead chew their shroud, and the family dies one by one. In 2009, archaeologists in Venice found a 16th-century woman buried with a brick in her mouth.

Would you have put the stone in? 👇
