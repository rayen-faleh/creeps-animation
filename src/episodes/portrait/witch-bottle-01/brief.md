# The Witch Bottle — Part I (brief)

- Series: THE WITCH BOTTLE, four parts, one real 17th-century witch test per part (`series/witch-brainstorm.md`,
  round 2, approved). Mardle Fen, East Anglia, winter 1645. First person, past tense (a man remembering being nine).
- Voices: Kokoro `bm_lewis` narrator; whisper "It burns." in `bf_emma` (Goody Ashe at the door).
- Test: the witch bottle (research.md R1-R3).
- Closeness: the town (three small graves in the lane).
- Ladder: why boil pins? → who is "someone"? → what goes in the jug (and whose grey hair?) → the rule → will she come?
  → the baby stops coughing (worse or better?) → the knock → open it.
- Clues for later parts: the long grey hair from a comb the narrator has never seen (Gran put Goody Ashe's hair in the
  bottle: paid off in Part IV); Gran doesn't move at the knock.
- Red words: `someone.` (Gran's accusation), `knocked.`
- Cliffhanger: my hand lifts the latch; a hand bound in smoking rag curls through the gap; hard cut; "It burns."
