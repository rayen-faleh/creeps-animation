# My Type, Part 1 (brief)

- A vampire horror-comedy series, ~65 s plus a poll card, 9:16, narrated by `bm_lewis`. Her whisper is `bf_emma`.
  Brainstorm: `series/my-type.md`.
- Premise: Hollywood gave him a type (pale, black lace, hates mornings). After eight years of goth nights he finally
  meets a real vampire. A broken wine glass leads to an accidental bite, and now the city's vampire council votes on
  whether he lives.
- Hook: a flash-forward to the council table ("Tonight, seven vampires vote on whether I get to live"), then the comic
  flashback.
- Twist: the crime isn't the bite. His selfie story from that night shows two glasses and nobody next to him, and 312
  people watched it. That breaks the council's rule: "no one finds out".
- Clues before the twist:
  - Spoken: "Not drinking." and her asking to be invited in.
  - Visual: the selfie is seen while it's being taken, with her glass in mid-air.
- Ending: the vote is three each and the last vote is hers. Her whisper "Sorry." echoes "She said sorry. She said it a lot."
- Poll card: HOW DOES SHE VOTE? A = Spare him, B = Stake him. Part 2 plays the winner.
